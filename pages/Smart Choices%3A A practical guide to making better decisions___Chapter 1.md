- ### Making Decisions is a fundamental Life skill
- ### You can learn to make better decisions
    - An Effective [[Decision Making]] process fulfills these criteria:
        - It focuses on what's **important**
        - It's **logical** and **consistent**
        - It acknowledges both **subjective** and **objective** factors and blends **analytical** with **intuitive** thinking.
        - It **requires** only as much information and analysis as is **necessary** to resolve a particular dilemma
        - It encourages and **guides** the **gathering** of relevant **information** and informed opinion.
        - It is **straightforward**, **reliable**, **easy to use** and **flexible**.
- ### Use the PrOACT Approach to make Smart Choices
    - The essence of PrOACT approach is to **divide and conquer**.
    - The Eight elements of Smart Choices
        - The First 5 are core to any decision making process
            - **Pr**oblem
            - **O**bjectives
            - **A**lternatives
            - **C**onsequences
            - **T**rade-offs
        - The remaining 3 elements Help clarify decisions in a volatile or evolving environments. Some simple decisions may not involve these
            - Uncertainty
            - Risk Tolerance
            - Linked Decisions
