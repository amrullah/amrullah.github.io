- This mental model is about noticing the trends in progress.
- When you first start something (0 to 1) the progress is infinity. But as you go from 1 to 2, 2 to 3 and so on, the rate of progress slows down.
- Somewhere around there's an equilibrium point which represents what the average progress rate will be.
