- ## What is Example Mapping?
  
  Example Mapping is a method designed to help teams explore and understand the problem domain by using **concrete examples** to define and clarify user stories and their acceptance criteria. It is commonly used in [[Agile]] , [[BDD]] , and [[TDD]] practices to foster collaboration between business [[stakeholders]] and development teams, ensuring everyone shares the same understanding of the story and its requirements #definition
- ## Benefits of Example Mapping
- **Clarifies acceptance criteria**: Ensures the team understands what “done” means for a story
- **Encourages collaboration**: Involves business users, developers, and testers in a shared conversation
- **Identifies gaps and edge cases**: Red cards highlight uncertainties or missing information
- **Prevents last-minute surprises**: Helps break down large stories into manageable pieces and avoids over-complexity
- **Supports test creation**: Examples can be directly converted into automated tests or living documentation
