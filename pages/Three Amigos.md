- In **Agile software development**, the **Three Amigos** refers to a collaborative meeting that brings together three key perspectives: **business (business analyst or product owner), development (developers), and testing (quality assurance analysts)**
  
  
  #+BEGIN_QUOTE
  The purpose is to ensure a shared understanding of requirements, clarify ambiguities, and define acceptance criteria before development begins
  #+END_QUOTE
- Key aspects include:
    - ** [[Business Analyst]] :** Represents customer requirements and expectations, ensuring the team understands what needs to be built.
    - **Developer:** Provides technical feasibility insights and discusses potential solutions or features
    - **Tester/QA Analyst:** Focuses on [[testability]], quality, and acceptance criteria to ensure the solution meets agreed standards
- The **Three Amigos meeting** helps identify misunderstandings early, fosters alignment with business goals, and improves the efficiency and quality of software delivery
