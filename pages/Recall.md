- Trying to explain the [[Concept]] you learnt freshly, to someone.
- Or can also do this solo, by just looking away and trying to recall the key ideas in the material you just learnt.
- Helps check if the [[Concept]] is properly lodged in [[LTM]]
- Is a better alternative to other approaches like:
    - rereading
    - underlining or highlighting
    - concept mapping
